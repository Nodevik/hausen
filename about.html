Our Story

Crafting Walls
Into Masterpieces

Hausen was born from a singular belief — that walls deserve more than paint. We engineer premium Super Heavy PVC panels that transform every interior into a statement of refined taste and lasting quality.

Our Journey

Where Vision Meets
Precision Engineering

Hausen started with a simple question: why settle for ordinary walls? Founded in 2019, we set out to redefine interior surfaces with panels that combine architectural beauty with industrial-grade durability.

Our Super Heavy PVC panels — each precisely 5mm thick and 3050mm long — are engineered to exacting standards, offering 15 premium finishes from rich wood grains to dramatic marbles and ornate patterns.

Today, Hausen panels grace homes, offices, hotels, and commercial spaces — everywhere that quality and aesthetics matter. Every panel is a testament to our commitment: surfaces that inspire for a lifetime.
